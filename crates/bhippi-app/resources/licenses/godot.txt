Third-party components inside Godot
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Godot build Bhippi ships contains third-party libraries under their own
licences, including FreeType, HarfBuzz, Thorvg, Brotli, miniupnpc, mbedTLS,
Zstandard, libwebp, libogg, libvorbis, wslay and others. Each carries its own
copyright notice and permission text.

The authoritative, complete list for this exact build is the one the engine
itself reports:

    Godot editor → Help → About → Licenses

and the COPYRIGHT.txt shipped with the official release at
https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4.7.1-stable/COPYRIGHT.txt
